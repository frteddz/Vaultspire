THE LOOP

three rules, whole season

VAULTS

Inventory slots are the life pool. Stacked players turn clumsy, broke players turn sneaky.

SEALS

Locked slots plate over. No dupes, no drops, no bypass. Death and /clear reseal on their own.

SPIRES

Hidden lodestone spires sell buyouts. Pay up, name the broke player, confirm.

GEAR SEALS

thresholds in config

RANDOM PICK, EVERY TIME

At 12, 9, 6, 3 and 1 vaults left, one more slot seals at random from helmet, chest, legs, boots, offhand. The piece pops off. Climb back up and seals open in reverse. Cheap revives come back missing gear.

COMMANDS

/vaultYour open vaults and sealed gear.
/vaultguiFull menu. Status, spires, map, pack, admin tools.
/vault helpEvery command, admin ones too if you are op.
/vault creditsAuthor, version, art notes.
/vault packPack install help, in game.
/spiremapMap to spires within 1000 blocks. 20s cooldown.
/spirerandomScatter menu for ops. Amount plus per-biome presets.
/spirerandom 5 1Scatter direct. 5 spires, 1 per split biome.
/vault set / addAdmin vault edits. Logged to console.
/vault spire addAdmin manual spire where you stand, cheap or pricey.

INSTALL

four moves

  1. Drop vaultspire-1.0.2.jar into plugins and restart. Player data migrates to playerdata.yml by itself.
  2. As op run /spirerandom, open the scatter GUI, hit Quick 5x1. Check with /spiremap.
  3. Set the resource pack lines in server.properties to the URL and sha1 below.
  4. Restart, join, accept the pack. Open /vaultgui. Seals should wear the vault plating.
resource-pack=https://frteddz.github.io/Vaultspire/vaultspire-pack.zip
resource-pack-sha1=67ef259577522e0c8334b4d2afb6d06b01749a37
