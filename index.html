PAPER 1.21.11 / SMP CORE

LOSE SLOTS.
NOT HEARTS.

An SMP core where inventory slots are lives. Steal them, seal them, buy them back.

Download 1.0.3
01

THE LOOP

VAULTS

Inventory slots are the life pool. Stacked players turn clumsy, broke players turn sneaky.

SEALS

Locked slots plate over in vanilla glass. No dupes, no drops, no bypass.

SPIRES

Hidden lodestone spires sell buyouts. Pay up, name the broke player, confirm.

02

BUYOUTS

TWO TIERS, ONE COOLDOWN

Cheap spires cost less and guard harder. Pricey spires cost more and guard lighter. Every spire rests 90 minutes after a buyout, so control of the map matters.

Gear seals bite as vaults fall: at 12, 9, 6, 3 and 1 vaults left another armor or offhand slot seals at random. Thresholds live in the config.

03

COMMANDS

/vaultFull menu. Status, spires, map, admin tools.
/vault helpEvery command, admin ones too if you are op.
/vault creditsAuthor, version, site.
/vault revive <player>Admin instant revive, works on yourself too.
/spiremapMap to spires within 1000 blocks. 20s cooldown.
/spirerandomScatter menu for ops. Amount plus per-biome presets.
/spirerandom 5 1Scatter direct. 5 spires, 1 per split biome.
/vault set / addAdmin vault edits. Logged to console.
/vault spire addAdmin manual spire where you stand, cheap or pricey.
04

INSTALL

  1. Download vaultspire-1.0.3.jar from the downloads page and drop it into plugins.
  2. Restart. As op run /spirerandom, hit Quick 5x1, check with /spiremap.
